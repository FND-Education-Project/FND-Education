**Free, evidence-informed education about Functional Neurological Disorder (FND) for people with FND, the people who support them, and clinicians.**

> **Important:** This project is under active development. It provides general education, not individual medical advice, diagnosis, emergency assessment, or treatment.

## Start here

### Course

The **[FND Course](course/README.md)** is a step-by-step introduction to FND. It covers what FND is, how it is diagnosed, current research and uncertainty, symptom knowledge, safety, treatment and rehabilitation, daily life, self-advocacy, and long-term management.

### Reference Library

The **[Reference Library](reference/README.md)** is for looking up a symptom, clinical question, recovery approach, or related condition. It includes:

- **[Understanding an FND diagnosis](reference/diagnostic-concepts/README.md)** — shared explanations of positive diagnosis, tests and investigations, everyday function, and other concepts that recur across symptoms.
- **[Diagnostic techniques](reference/diagnostic-signs/README.md)** — symptom-specific positive signs, examination techniques, diagnostic criteria, limitations, and differential considerations.
- **[Recovery techniques](reference/recovery-techniques/README.md)** — symptom-specific rehabilitation, treatment, self-management, safety, access, and continuing-care approaches.
- **[Common co-occurring symptoms and conditions](reference/co-occurring-conditions/README.md)** — topics such as migraine, persistent headache, tinnitus, pain, fatigue, sleep difficulty, autonomic symptoms, and other conditions that may occur alongside FND.

The **[glossary](glossary/README.md)** explains clinical, research, and rehabilitation terms in plain language.

For shorter printable material, visit **[Booklets](booklets/README.md)** and **[Course Puzzles](assets/puzzles/README.md)**.

## Three main audiences

Most educational pages are organized for three audiences:

1. **For the Person With FND**
2. **For Family, Friends, and Other Supporters**
3. **For Clinicians and the Care Team**

A **Research and Sources** section supports those three audiences with citations, evidence limits, and source notes.
