Start with assessment and one approach that fits your care plan. You do not need to practise everything. Cough can protect the airway; these pages are not instructions to suppress choking, productive cough or unexplained breathing difficulty. Functional cough, tic cough, somatic cough syndrome and inducible laryngeal obstruction require distinct assessment. ILO is not automatically FND.
