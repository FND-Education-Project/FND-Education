This folder expands the brief technique descriptions on the [functional limb weakness recovery page](../01-functional-limb-weakness.md). Each page explains which appearance of weakness may fit the technique, the relevant anatomy in clinical and everyday language, how a clinician may select and progress it, how supporters can help, and where the evidence stops.

## Original list: 10 entries

10 original entries became 12 pages by separating backward walking from foot sliding, and mirror feedback from treadmill/body-weight support. No extra list entry was needed. These are document counts, not counts of independently proven treatments. [See the history audit](../../../docs/project/recovery-technique-history-audit.md).

1. **Automatic, task-oriented movement:** [Automatic and Task-Oriented Movement for Functional Limb Weakness](01-automatic-and-task-oriented-movement.md).
2. **External focus or divided attention:** [External Focus and Divided Attention for Functional Limb Weakness](02-external-focus-and-divided-attention.md).
3. **Early supported weight-bearing:** [Supported Loading and Weight-Bearing for Functional Limb Weakness](03-supported-loading-and-weight-bearing.md).
4. **Build a step from weight shift:** [Step Initiation From Weight Shift for Functional Limb Weakness](04-step-initiation-from-weight-shift.md).
5. **Backward walking or foot sliding:** [Foot Sliding for Ankle and Leg Weakness](05-foot-sliding-for-ankle-and-leg-weakness.md); [Backward Walking as a Bridge to Forward Walking](06-backward-walking-as-a-bridge.md).
6. **Treadmill, mirror or body-weight support:** [Treadmill and Body-Weight-Supported Walking](07-treadmill-and-body-weight-supported-walking.md); [Mirror and Visual Feedback for Functional Limb Weakness](08-mirror-and-visual-feedback.md).
7. **Meaningful arm and two-handed tasks:** [Meaningful Upper-Limb and Two-Handed Tasks](09-meaningful-upper-limb-and-two-handed-tasks.md).
8. **Graded activity and reconditioning:** [Graded Activity and Reconditioning for Functional Limb Weakness](10-graded-activity-and-reconditioning.md).
9. **Electrical stimulation as an adjunct:** [Electrical Stimulation as a Movement Adjunct](11-electrical-stimulation-as-an-adjunct.md).
10. **Relapse and self-management plan:** [Relapse and Self-Management Planning for Functional Limb Weakness](12-relapse-and-self-management-planning.md).

These are components a qualified clinician can combine, adapt or omit. They are not a fixed sequence. A technique helping or not helping does not confirm or disprove FND. New or substantially changed weakness needs appropriate assessment.
