# Swallowing and globus: evidence-presentation review

October 10, 2026. Internal editorial record; excluded from the website and search. Overview, three detailed pages and seven-entry inventory reviewed. Targeted review, not a systematic review or clinical approval.

## Source decisions and access

- Baker 2021 (FND-CIT-0025): long supplementary version checked for positive functional assessment, globus and swallowing. Oropharyngeal assessment, oesophageal disorders of gut–brain interaction and globus are kept distinct.
- Rome Foundation Rome V A4/A5 (0248) checked directly. Existing duration, symptom and exclusion paraphrases retained. The eight-week optimized antisecretory-treatment footnote belongs to globus; it is not general self-treatment advice or a reason to delay assessment. Ineffective oesophageal motility is not treated as one of the excluded major motor disorders.
- Gyawali 2026 (0249): primary indexed abstract and bibliographic metadata checked; full-text appraisal unavailable. Rome V criteria themselves supply the public criteria statements. The inventory now cites Rome V directly rather than only its 2016 predecessor.
- Aziz 2016 (0164): volume/pages corrected to 150(6):1368–1379 using institutional metadata. Retain as historical Rome IV provenance, not current criteria. Age alone is not grounds for unlinking.
- ASHA Adult Dysphagia, FEES and VFSS (0115, 0250, 0251) checked directly. FEES whiteout/visibility and VFSS sampling/radiation limits retained; a normal sample cannot independently establish FND. Instrument-specific sources added to the inventory.
- Lu 2022 (0252): primary indexed abstract checked; full-text retrieval unavailable. Ninety-six selected Rome-IV-defined motility patients and healthy comparators. Questionnaire associations do not establish cause, compulsory psychiatric disorder or validation of motor-FND signs. Public access notes moved here.

## Currentness search

Targeted October 10 searches covered functional dysphagia/globus and 2025–2026 guidance. Anderson and colleagues' Multidisciplinary Assessment and Management of Functional Dysphagia (2026 issue; online 2025; DOI 10.1016/j.gastha.2025.100762) was identified. Publisher/institutional abstract and metadata checked; full text unavailable. It describes a multidisciplinary approach and uses Rome IV; it does not supersede current Rome V or validate a diagnostic battery. Retained as an internal full-text candidate.
