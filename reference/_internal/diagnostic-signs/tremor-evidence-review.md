## Presentation decisions

Lead clinical reading with the 2024 IFCN chapter (FND-CIT-0022) and diagnostic synthesis with the June 2026 meta-analysis (FND-CIT-0224). Bring both into the inventory and its source-use map. Preserve all twelve headings, existing source IDs, local citation anchors, three Quick Reference links, audience sections and the distinction between the two detailed comparisons.

Keep public limits that affect interpretation: task performance, mixed tremor, body-site applicability, single-sign versus complete-battery evidence, and everyday function. Move search/access bookkeeping here. Retain the primary 2016 figures rather than copying a discrepancy from the later review. Retain the small 2025 series as exploratory, not as stronger evidence merely because it is newer.

## Currentness search

October 7 searches used “functional tremor diagnosis electrophysiology 2025 2026 review validation”, “clinical neurophysiology functional motor disorders IFCN handbook 2024 tremor”, and targeted primary-paper searches. Publisher/PubMed records were preferred. This was not an exhaustive database review; absence from these results does not establish absence of later evidence.

The 2026 meta-analysis was already cited in the detailed drafts, so no duplicate central source was created. This pass changes its prominence and inventory coverage.

Two additional items are linked once in the reading guide:

- Cibula et al., 2026 conference abstract: 30 diagnosed participants, with shortened-battery analysis restricted to the 24 positive on the full battery. No comparison group to establish specificity. Do not replace the established protocol with this exploratory shortcut.
- “Functional Tremor”, PMID 41397453: newer review record identified, but full text and complete metadata were not obtained. Do not import its mechanistic language as settled explanation or substitute it for the diagnostic-accuracy synthesis.

A 2026 split-belt gait-adaptation study also appeared in the search. It concerns a possible mechanism in people with tremor, not validation of the bedside comparisons, and was not added to these diagnostic pages.
