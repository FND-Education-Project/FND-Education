# Functional paralysis evidence-presentation review

Internal editorial record, October 12, 2026. Covers the overview, three detailed guides and nine-entry inventory. This is a presentation and targeted currentness review, not a systematic search or completed clinical appraisal.

## Source decisions

- Add the existing Dolbow 2025 technique review (0183) to all five pages. Publisher technique and interpretation sections checked, including finger abduction and the Spinal Injuries Center test. It brings together comparisons and their restrictions; it does not validate all paralysis presentations or measure disability. Its search ends March 2024. Reuse its stable ID rather than create a new record.
- Keep Bennett 2021 (0001) and the IFCN chapter (0022) in their clinical-framework and differential-investigation roles. Preserve care needs, coexistence and the boundary between seizure-confined immobility and limb symptoms outside events.
- Sonoo 2004 (0058) and Yugué 2004 (0166): apply the preceding limb-weakness decision consistently. Exact bibliographies, DOI and IDs remain plain text on these public pages. The contemporary review is the linked explanation. Sonoo's intentional-deception framing was checked in the earlier pass; Yugué's primary indexed record was rechecked here, without claiming complete full-text appraisal. Original access links remain once in the internal guide.
- Baker and Silver 1987 (0120): primary indexed abstract checked. Its proposed diagnostic features include psychiatric history, employment and compensation claims. Keep the historical citation unlinked on reviewed public pages and lead with positive movement findings. Normal tone/reflexes do not establish FND. This decision concerns substantive diagnostic framing, not age alone.
- Tinazzi 2008 (0057) and Lombardi 2014 (0134): retain their links and bounded small-sample findings. Cross-check interpretation against Dolbow. Preserve complete unilateral paralysis versus partial weakness and bilateral applicability limits; do not convert perfect classification in selected samples into universal accuracy. No new full-text appraisal of these primary studies claimed.
- Other existing sources retain their established roles and identifiers. Emergency guidance and cauda equina evidence are not positive motor-sign validation. No new force, duration, disability or meaningful-change threshold is introduced.
