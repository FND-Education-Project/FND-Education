## Targeted search and newer candidates

Web-indexed PubMed/publisher searches on October 7 used “functional limb weakness diagnostic signs 2025 2026 systematic review”, the diagnostic-accuracy review title, and hip-abductor/Hoover terms. Original publisher and PubMed records were preferred. Search results do not establish completeness or the absence of later replication.

- Patwal and colleagues, 2023 systematic review: title, authors and journal record identified. Publisher access blocked; full appraisal outstanding. High-priority next source check before adopting pooled estimates.
- 2025 BMJ Neurology Open exploratory study in post-acute SARS-CoV-2 illness: indexed abstract identified; publisher/PMC full-text retrieval blocked. Selected-population research is not automatically general diagnostic-accuracy validation. Do not extend its findings to all limb weakness.
- Ishihara and colleagues, January 14, 2026 hip-abductor case report: bibliographic record identified; full text blocked and no abstract available. A case illustration cannot replace diagnostic-accuracy validation. Suitability of the article’s language is not yet assessed.

These candidates are linked once in the reading guide, not added as public supporting citations or assigned central IDs before use. Recent publication alone does not establish better quality or appropriate framing.
