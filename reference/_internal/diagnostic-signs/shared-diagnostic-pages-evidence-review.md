## Cauda equina source appraisal

GIRFT March 2026 PDF accessed from the programme's official site; checked its emergency referral, bladder-scan and imaging sections. Current guidance, not FND diagnostic validation. Public text keeps only the decision-relevant point that a low post-void residual or normal bedside finding cannot independently exclude CES. Does not export England-specific operational targets as universal rules or add catheter manoeuvres.

Hoeritzauer 2021 prospective cohort: primary abstract and accepted manuscript retrieved from UCL. Consecutive regional neurosurgical cohort of 198; scan-positive, mixed root-compression and scan-negative groups are distinct. Alternative neurological diagnoses were identified during admission and follow-up. Functional signs occurred across categories. This supports individual assessment and follow-up, not automatic FND diagnosis after a negative scan or proof of a psychological mechanism. Neurological examination was not a blinded universal diagnostic test; ascertainment and referral selection limit generalization. No new percentage-based diagnostic rule is added.

Earlier 2018 retrospective cohort retained as supporting history. Further emergency, spinal, neurourology and pelvic-floor specialist review remains pending. Targeted currentness search included prospective CES cohorts and official GIRFT guidance; later machine-learning and service-flow studies do not replace clinical emergency assessment.
