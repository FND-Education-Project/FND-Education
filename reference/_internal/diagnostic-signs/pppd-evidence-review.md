# PPPD evidence-presentation review

Internal editorial record, October 11, 2026. Covers the symptom overview, three detailed guides and eight-entry inventory. Human and specialist review remain pending; this targeted review is not a systematic search or a claim that every paper has been fully appraised.

## Decisions

- Staab 2017 (0027) remains the diagnostic authority. The Bárány Society's current ICVD list still lists this consensus; no replacement criteria were identified. Retain the linked source despite its age. Preserve all five criteria, coexistence, and the distinction between positive history and differential investigations. Criterion E now consistently reads “no better explanation”; it does not demand exhaustive normal tests.
- Yagi 2019 (0156) remains the instrument-development source. Existing format, scoring and selected-sample accuracy discussion retained. Do not replace the instrument with a locally rewritten questionnaire.
- Meletaki 2024 (0262): publisher methods/results checked. Retain the French validation's limited between-patient discrimination and the distinction between internal consistency and diagnostic accuracy. Added directly to the inventory bibliography, already present in overview and measurement guide.
- Kim, Park and Kim 2026 (new 0271): online September 23, 2025, assigned to May 2026 issue. Publisher abstract and bibliographic metadata checked. Korean NPQ study: 255 participants, 150 PPPD, 64 peripheral and 41 central vestibular diagnoses; total-score AUC 0.670. Added to overview, measurement guide and inventory. Only abstract-supported findings used. Full methods and supplement appraisal pending; no new clinical cutoff or combined NPQ/DHI calculator adopted.
- Keep the authored everyday activity record separate from a validated measure, diagnostic criteria and occupational-capacity decisions. No new disability or meaningful-change threshold.

## Currentness and access

Checked the official ICVD consensus list, the 2024 French publisher text and the 2026 Korean publisher abstract. Targeted searches covered PPPD criteria and questionnaire validation through October 11, 2026. Later management reviews and therapy cohorts do not supersede diagnostic criteria or validate a diagnostic score. The Korean study's references also identify other language adaptations; this pass is not an exhaustive psychometric review of all versions.

The reading guide retains one access link per paper, including removal of the second NPQ access link via PMID. Search/access notes stay here rather than adding public warnings. The differential guide needed only wording cleanup; its core assessment framework remains unchanged. All eight entries, three Quick Reference links, headings, share anchors and media briefs retained. No archived human accounts restored.
