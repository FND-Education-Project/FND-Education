# Functional cognitive disorder: evidence-presentation review

October 11, 2026. Internal editorial record; excluded from website/search. Overview, four detailed pages and inventory reviewed. Targeted review, not a systematic review or clinical approval.

## Diagnostic evidence

- Ball 2020 (0071) remains the positive same-domain internal-inconsistency framework. Prior full-text appraisal retained. Coexistence, access barriers, differential assessment and everyday disability remain explicit.
- McWhirter 2022 (0026): primary abstract rechecked; institutional manuscript fetch timed out. Retain development-model scope (49 participants, 31 FCD); do not treat age or response length as independent proof. Full-method reappraisal remains pending.
- Ball 2021 (0141): primary indexed abstract rechecked; publisher full-text fetch failed. Prior expansion methods appraisal retained. The 21/17/25 clinic comparison does not supply a universal neuropsychological cutoff. Validity findings do not determine motive or diagnose FCD.
- Cabreira 2025 (0140): publisher PDF via Edinburgh checked, including retrospective extraction and limitations. Clinicians knew diagnosis; missing-record assumptions and selected services limit transfer. Keep both checklist versions separate from FCD-Q8 and retain need for prospective blinded external validation.
- García-Roldán 2025 (0260): publisher full text checked, including methods and limitations. Existing sample, AUC, cutoff and accuracy figures confirmed. Selected biomarker-enriched phase-1 groups and moderate internal consistency limit generalization. This does not validate the Cabreira checklist or quantify disability.
- Mammì et al. 2026 (new 0270): publisher-indexed metadata, highlights and available preview checked. Online October 8, journal pre-proof; search through January 9, 2025. Full-text appraisal unavailable. Public use is limited to the reported synthesis of 23 studies, heterogeneous profiles and lack of standalone cognitive-test specificity. No detailed methods or new accuracy claim inferred. Recheck final publication metadata later.

Baum 2008 appraisal remains abstract-level from the earlier expansion; remove that access note from public prose, retain the stroke-population limit. No universal FCD meaningful-change threshold added.

## Currentness and follow-up

October 11 searches covered diagnostic tools, neuropsychological synthesis and everyday measurement. Cabreira et al.'s 2023 systematic review (10.1016/j.jpsychores.2023.111447) was accessed via Edinburgh; it supplies background for later checklist work, not a mandate to withhold supporters or aids.
