# Cough and upper-airway: evidence-presentation review

October 11, 2026. Internal editorial record; excluded from website/search. Overview, three detailed pages and seven-entry inventory reviewed. Targeted review, not a systematic review or clinical approval.

## Source decisions

- Baker 2021 (0025) remains the functional communication/swallowing/cough consensus framework, read in the preceding pass. Retain the boundary between this broad framework and respiratory cough hypersensitivity, tic/somatic cough criteria and ILO. An unexplained persistent cough is not itself a positive FND diagnosis.
- BTS 2023 (0254): official final statement PDF checked, including assessment, hypersensitivity and life impact. The BTS resource still marks this statement valid. Adult scope retained. Add the source to the inventory's differential investigation entry.
- CHEST 2015 (0129): recommendations and study counts rechecked in primary indexed material; earlier expansion full-text review retained. Keep the link despite historical terms in the title: its actual recommendations reject anxiety/depression, cough sound and sleep absence as diagnostic shortcuts. Eighteen uncontrolled studies, 223 participants, 96% children/adolescents; no adult diagnostic-accuracy claim. No source is removed solely because it is old.
- Haines 2020 (0147): retained as historical pandemic-era consensus, with current assessment led by Leong 2023 and ASHA. Pandemic contingency pathways are not routine substitutes for laryngoscopy.
- Leong 2023 (0255): primary abstract/metadata rechecked, not full methodological reappraisal. Consensus remains distinct from independent accuracy validation. Access note moved off public pages. Original numerical consensus statements retained.
- ASHA ILO guidance (new 0269): official page checked directly. Supports multidisciplinary differential assessment and laryngoscopy during relevant provocation, with CLE for exercise-related symptoms. It does not classify all ILO as neurological FND. Added to overview, laryngoscopy page and inventory.
- Malaty 2022 (0110) retained for the functional tic-like classification boundary; not a cough-specific study. Clinical cough and tic/somatic detailed pages required no substantive changes.

## Currentness and follow-up

October 11 searches covered 2025–2026 cough/ILO assessment. BTS paediatric material located was a draft/under-development statement, not adopted as final guidance. Newer German adult guidance and ASHA clinical primers do not automatically supersede the claim-matched sources.

Two later primary ILO papers were located: Stojanovic et al. on mannitol provocation (2025; DOI 10.1016/j.anai.2025.02.001), and Wong et al. on interrater reliability (2026; DOI 10.1016/j.jaip.2025.11.034). Metadata/available primary summaries were checked, but complete appraisal remains pending. The latter follows a 2025 conference abstract; do not mistake the abstract for the final report or transfer its numbers without checking. No new provocation protocol or accuracy estimate added. A 2026 patient-reported ILO measure-development study is a measurement follow-up candidate, not diagnostic validation.
