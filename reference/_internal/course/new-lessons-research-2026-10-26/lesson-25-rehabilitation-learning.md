# Research brief — proposed Lesson 25: How Rehabilitation Uses Learning

**October 26, 2026 · Internal authoring material · Not a published lesson**

[Brief index](README.md) · [Source register](sources.md)

## Teaching purpose and explanations to develop

Neuroplasticity is nervous-system adaptation with experience. It is not a promise that understanding a concept, practising harder or changing thoughts will remove symptoms. Separate the rationale for learning-based care from trial evidence for a particular programme and outcome.

## Researched foundation and its limits

[R12] General motor-learning neuroscience concerns experience-related change, not an FND cure. [R13] Physio4FMD randomized 355 adults with functional motor disorder: specialist versus usual neurological physiotherapy did not differ significantly on the primary physical-function outcome at 12 months; some secondary outcomes favoured specialist care. [R14] Speech/swallowing guidance is consensus for different clinical presentations.

Sources: [R12](sources.md#r12), [R13](sources.md#r13), [R14](sources.md#r14). See the register for source type, access limits and publication details. These are drafting claims, not a completed independent clinical audit.

## Material for the three audience sections

Person: A rehabilitation goal is joining a family meal. The team first asks whether the barrier is transfers, speech, swallowing, fatigue or several together. The scene shows why the same general idea of learning does not produce one exercise for everyone. Explain treatment roles before discussing practice.

Supporter: Notice access and participation gains without judging symptoms or effort. Do not compare one person's outcome to another's.

Clinician: Match profession and intervention to assessed needs. A rehabilitation package does not isolate its active mechanism; selected trial participants do not represent every severity or comorbidity. Distinguish a transient clinic response from sustained benefit.

## Reflection, application and boundaries

Optional question: “What daily activity would I want care to help with?” Reading order is not a treatment prerequisite. Preserve care when improvement is limited. Do not claim that structural injury is necessary for plasticity or that normal routine scans prove a prognosis.
