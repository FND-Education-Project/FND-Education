# Research brief — proposed Lesson 26: Choosing and Adjusting Meaningful Practice

**October 26, 2026 · Internal authoring material · Not a published lesson**

## Teaching purpose and explanations to develop

Start with what the person wants to do, then identify the activity's demands, useful help, tolerable practice and review point. A personally meaningful task is a goal-setting choice, not a guarantee of faster neural change. Sustainable participation may include another person or equipment.

## Researched foundation and its limits

[R01] Motor consensus supports functional task-oriented retraining. [R15] OT consensus supports activity analysis and collaborative goals, but does not validate every practical adaptation. The source's concerns about equipment require individual interpretation; the project does not use them as a blanket reason to deny access.

Sources: [R01](sources.md#r01), [R15](sources.md#r15). See the register for source type, access limits and publication details. These are drafting claims, not a completed independent clinical audit.

## Material for the three audience sections

Person: A seated craft activity matters because it allows time with a grandchild. Compare three fictional plans: long repetitions, a shorter shared activity with materials nearby, and postponing while symptoms are unusually severe. Explain what each plan asks of movement, attention and recovery. Let the useful outcome be time together rather than a perfect movement.

Supporter: Agree what help is welcome and what is manageable for the supporter. An invitation to help is not an appointment as exercise supervisor.

Clinician: Define the selected task, setup, cue, assistance, stop/review criteria and meaningful outcome. Address falls, swallowing, respiratory or other hazards with appropriate expertise. Review delayed as well as immediate effects.

## Reflection, application and boundaries

Optional planning conversation: “What would make this activity easier to access, and how would we know whether the plan fits?” No default repetitions, fixed progression or unsupervised risky transfer. Aids and treatment may coexist. Pausing or changing a plan is an acceptable result.
