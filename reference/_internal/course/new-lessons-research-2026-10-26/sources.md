# Source register — Lessons 16–30 research briefs

**Checked October 26, 2026.** Internal source keys below are not new permanent citation IDs. Reuse established IDs where confirmed in the earlier review; check the public index before allocating any new one. These briefs synthesize research for drafting; they are not a systematic review or independent clinical sign-off.

## Retrieval and interpretation

Research was checked against original journal articles, their PubMed records and official guidance. Access was uneven: some direct requests returned a browser challenge, an empty page or HTTP 403. Abstracts and search-indexed original text supported the narrow claims recorded below; no inaccessible full text is represented as fully appraised. Existing source notes in the [prior review](../syllabus-redesign-review-2026-10-26.md#checked-source-kit) remain useful. Before final publication, inspect exact supporting paragraphs and methods for any expanded claim, verify corrections, and register source use in the established index.

General neuroscience, FND observations, mechanism models, consensus and efficacy trials answer different questions. Original teaching scenes are explicitly fictional. An editorial access policy is not attributed to a trial. No individual prognosis, effect-size calculation or universal symptom mechanism is inferred.

## R01

Nielsen G, Stone J, Matthews A, et al. **Physiotherapy for functional motor disorders: a consensus recommendation.** JNNP. 2015;86:1113–1119. doi:10.1136/jnnp-2014-309255. [Original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4602268/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25433033/). Existing **FND-CIT-0028**.

Professional consensus, not an efficacy trial. Indexed original text/abstract checked. Supports task-oriented motor retraining; does not establish that an exercise works across FND presentations. Older language and equipment recommendations need context, individual safety assessment and the project's continuing-access policy. Used in 16, 17, 26.

## R02

Huys ACML, Haggard P, Bhatia KP, Edwards MJ. **Misdirected attentional focus in functional tremor.** Brain. 2021;144:3436–3450. doi:10.1093/brain/awab230. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34145898/) · [Original](https://academic.oup.com/brain/article/144/11/3436/6305827). Existing **FND-CIT-0220**.

Primary task experiment in selected action-tremor groups, with healthy and other action-tremor comparators; abstract/indexed original checked, direct PMC access challenged. Supports task-dependent attention findings, not a universal diagnostic rule or generalized treatment prescription. Used in 16–17.

## R03

Edwards MJ, Adams RA, Brown H, Parees I, Friston KJ. **A Bayesian account of 'hysteria'.** Brain. 2012;135:3495–3512. doi:10.1093/brain/aws129. [Original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3501967/). Existing **FND-CIT-0242**, as recorded in the prior review.

Theoretical paper; indexed original abstract checked. Preserve the historical title only for accurate attribution. Proposed Bayesian explanation is not established individual causation. Used in 18.

## R04

Drane DL, Fani N, Hallett M, Khalsa SS, Perez DL, Roberts NA. **A framework for understanding the pathophysiology of functional neurological disorder.** CNS Spectr. 2021;26:555–561. doi:10.1017/S1092852920001789. [Original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7930164/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32883381/). Existing **FND-CIT-0006**.

Framework/synthesis; indexed original and abstract checked. Circuit and construct interactions remain incompletely understood. Useful clinician scaffolding for 18, 20, 21; no recipe for producing everyone's symptoms.

## R05

Khalsa SS, Adolphs R, Cameron OG, et al. **Interoception and mental health: a roadmap.** Biol Psychiatry Cogn Neurosci Neuroimaging. 2018;3:501–513. doi:10.1016/j.bpsc.2017.12.004. [Original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6054486/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29884281/).

General research roadmap/definitions, not an FND intervention trial. Abstract and indexed original checked. Defines sensing, integration and interpretation, including nonconscious processing; useful for distinguishing measures. Used in 19. Check whether already registered before assigning an ID.

## R06

Proske U, Gandevia SC. **The proprioceptive senses: their roles in signaling body shape, body position and movement, and muscle force.** Physiol Rev. 2012;92:1651–1697. doi:10.1152/physrev.00048.2011. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23073629/).

General physiology review; abstract/indexed record checked. Supports the proprioception definition, not a finding of impaired proprioception in all FND. Used in 19; registration pending index check.

## R07

Stoffel N, Mouthon M, Yang H, et al. **Attenuated heartbeat-evoked potentials in functional neurological disorder.** Brain Commun. 2026;8:fcaf503. doi:10.1093/braincomms/fcaf503. [Original](https://academic.oup.com/braincomms/article/8/1/fcaf503/8413452) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41550305/) · [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12810052/).

Primary cross-sectional cardiac/EEG study; abstract and indexed original methods/results/limitations checked. Do not equate questionnaires, EEG and task accuracy. Candidate markers require further validation; exploratory analyses and cardiac/measurement limitations matter. No transfer of breath-holding procedures into lessons. Used in 19; candidate registration pending.

## R08

Seeley WW, Menon V, Schatzberg AF, et al. **Dissociable intrinsic connectivity networks for salience processing and executive control.** J Neurosci. 2007;27:2349–2356. doi:10.1523/JNEUROSCI.5587-06.2007. [Original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2680293/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17329432/).

Primary general-neuroscience imaging study; indexed original/abstract checked. Introduces network distinctions, not a single brain “importance switch” or an FND biomarker. Used in 20; registration pending index check.

## R09

Voon V, Gallea C, Hattori N, et al. **The involuntary nature of conversion disorder.** Neurology. 2010;74:223–228. doi:10.1212/WNL.0b013e3181ca00e9. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20083798/).

Primary small motor/agency imaging study; abstract/indexed original checked. Historical terminology is not adopted. Findings do not classify an individual's agency, credibility or intent. Used in 21; registration pending index check.

## R10

Bennett K, Diamond C, Hoeritzauer I, et al. **A practical review of functional neurological disorder (FND) for the general physician.** Clin Med. 2021;21:28–36. doi:10.7861/clinmed.2020-0987. [Original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7850207/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33479065/). Existing **FND-CIT-0001**.

Clinical review; indexed original/abstract checked. Supports positive diagnosis and attention to alternative/coexisting conditions. It does not validate the fictional symptom-comparison exercise as a diagnostic instrument. Used in 22–24.

## R11

Tolchin B, Goldstein LH, Reuber M, et al. **Management of functional seizures practice guideline executive summary: report of the AAN Guidelines Subcommittee.** Neurology. 2026;106:e214466; online December 10, 2025. doi:10.1212/WNL.0000000000214466. [Original](https://www.neurology.org/doi/10.1212/WNL.0000000000214466) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41370742/). Existing **FND-CIT-0010**.

Professional guideline; PubMed abstract read directly and indexed original checked. Evidence search through February 25, 2025 identified 12 Class II–III studies. Recommends respectful explanation, shared care and assessment for coexisting epilepsy. Psychological interventions are described as possibly effective, not universally curative. Medication changes remain prescriber decisions. Used in 22–24, 27.

## R12

Dayan E, Cohen LG. **Neuroplasticity subserving motor skill learning.** Neuron. 2011;72:443–454. doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2011.10.008. [Original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3217208/).

General neuroscience review; indexed original checked. Supports the general relationship of learning and neural adaptation. It is not an FND efficacy trial or a basis for prescribing repetition/intensity in someone with delayed worsening. Used in 25; registration pending index check.

## R13

Nielsen G, Stone J, Lee TC, et al. **Specialist physiotherapy for functional motor disorder in England and Scotland (Physio4FMD): a pragmatic, multicentre, phase 3 randomised controlled trial.** Lancet Neurol. 2024;23:675–686. doi:10.1016/S1474-4422(24)00135-2. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38768621/). Existing **FND-CIT-0029**.

Primary randomized trial; indexed original abstract/results checked; direct PubMed open returned no substantive text. Preserve the primary outcome, comparator and time point. COVID interruptions excluded 89 randomized participants from the primary analysis; this and treatment selection limit interpretation. Secondary benefits do not erase the primary result, and the primary result does not prove physiotherapy ineffective. Used in 25, 27, 30.

## R14

Baker J, Barnett C, Cavalli L, et al. **Management of functional communication, swallowing, cough and related disorders: consensus recommendations for speech and language therapy.** JNNP. 2021;92:1112–1125. doi:10.1136/jnnp-2021-326767. [Original](https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/92/10/1112) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34210802/).

Professional consensus; abstract and indexed original checked. Distinguish this document from the accompanying editorial (PMID 34210803). Specialist assessment and symptomatic care are not permission for home swallowing/breathing experiments. Used in 25; check existing source ID before registration.

## R15

Nicholson C, Edwards MJ, Carson AJ, et al. **Occupational therapy consensus recommendations for functional neurological disorder.** JNNP. 2020;91:1037–1045. doi:10.1136/jnnp-2019-322281. [Original](https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/91/10/1037) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32732388/). Existing **FND-CIT-0011**.

Professional consensus; abstract/indexed original checked. Briefs use activity analysis, collaborative goals and context, not a validated pacing formula. Older cautions concerning aids are qualified by individual needs and project access policy. Fictional plans, supporter boundaries and reflection formats are editorial proposals rather than claimed trial findings. Used in 26, 28–30.

## R16

Goldstein LH, Robinson EJ, Mellers JDC, et al. **Cognitive behavioural therapy for adults with dissociative seizures (CODES): a pragmatic, multicentre, randomised controlled trial.** Lancet Psychiatry. 2020;7:491–505. doi:10.1016/S2215-0366(20)30128-0. [Original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7242906/) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32445688/).

Primary randomized trial; abstract and indexed original checked. Report the prespecified 12-month primary outcome alongside secondary benefits and the standardized-care comparator. It is one trial, not the whole contemporary guideline evidence base. Used in 27; check existing ID before registration.

## R17

NICE. **Myalgic encephalomyelitis (or encephalopathy)/chronic fatigue syndrome: diagnosis and management.** NG206, 2021. [Official recommendations](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/chapter/recommendations) · [Official guideline mirrored by NCBI](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK579533/). Existing **FND-CIT-0206** in the prior review.

Official condition-specific guidance, not an FND pacing trial. Indexed official recommendations checked, including 1.11.14; direct NICE request returned 403. Do not turn the prohibition on fixed incremental exercise for ME/CFS into a claim about every FND rehabilitation programme, or dismiss possible ME/CFS because FND is diagnosed. Used in 28–30 and the relevant boundary in 30; final publication should recheck current wording.

## Remaining editorial tasks

These notes do not establish an optimal rest interval, safe universal exercise dose, individual symptom mechanism, or successful adaptation. Obtain human review of new text, phenotype-appropriate clinical review where needed, complete citation/use records, and validate final links. Wider evidence searches are appropriate if Chat expands a claim beyond this register. No source has been assigned a fabricated permanent ID.
