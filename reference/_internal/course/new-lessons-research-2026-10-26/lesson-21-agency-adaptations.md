# Research brief — proposed Lesson 21: Sense of Agency, Learning, and Personal Adaptations

**October 26, 2026 · Internal authoring material · Not a published lesson**

[Brief index](README.md) · [Source register](sources.md)

## Teaching purpose and explanations to develop

Agency is the experience of initiating/controlling an action. Separate it from intention, responsibility, practical choice and actual ability. A person can choose a goal and still be unable to execute the action reliably. Ownership of a limb is related but distinct.

## Researched foundation and its limits

[R09] A small functional motor imaging study investigated altered agency-related processing. It supports a research question, not a universal brain signature or lie detector. [R04] Agency appears within a broader developing framework; it does not establish a single causal sequence.

Sources: [R09](sources.md#r09), [R04](sources.md#r04). See the register for source type, access limits and publication details. These are drafting claims, not a completed independent clinical audit.

## Material for the three audience sections

Person: A person wants to hand over a cup; an unwanted movement interrupts. Their intention is clear even though the movement is not under reliable control. Move to a planning scene: “Would a different container, help carrying it, or another way to participate be useful?” The adaptation is an original teaching proposal, not a studied agency treatment.

Supporter: Distinguish respecting a decision from expecting a movement to follow immediately. Ask before helping and allow the person to change their mind.

Clinician: Keep agency, intentional binding, explicit self-report and imaging measures separate. Do not translate group differences into individual culpability. Present any mechanism formulation as revisable.

## Reflection, application and boundaries

Integrate the concepts with a fictional worked example: understand the task → notice one obstacle → consider two options → choose a reversible adaptation → review usefulness. “Notice” need not mean self-monitoring or a diary. Review access, safety, strain and preference. An unsuccessful adaptation is information, not failed effort.
