- **Person:** One evolving fictional Natalie/Leah narrative across the three lessons; cheerful bakery callback and gentle humour without minimizing disability; a lovely afternoon can coexist with unchanged severe symptoms; curiosity rather than assumed fear in Lesson 25; reading the Library to prepare an informed question, **not self-diagnosis**, in Lesson 26.
