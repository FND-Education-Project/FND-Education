## Possible clinical/scientific accuracy questions for Work Audit 2 — no established error inferred from suspicion

1. **CBT evidence:** Independently check the CODES trial's enrolled population, standardized medical care, treatment frequency, statistically nonsignificant **12-month primary monthly-seizure** outcome, secondary seizure-free intervals and participation; LaFrance pilot scope and limitations; the 2026 **Re-PROGRAM/Higson trial** date, intervention naming, treatment arms, self-guided versus therapist-guided comparisons, confidence bounds and claim strength. Lesson 37's page-local `[5]` currently awaits full journal metadata and permanent central citation ID; reconcile with study evidence and index before publication.
2. **Psychological treatment rationale and safety:** Gutkin 2021 psychotherapy review populations/outcomes and quality, BPS 2024 guidance versus randomized evidence, DSM diagnostic framing, and how to distinguish possible maintaining processes/psychological comorbidity from presumed historical cause. Ensure Nadia's changing migraine remains separately assessed; do not infer a clinical error simply because a fictional clinician asks about emotion.
3. **Communication aids and consent:** Baker et al. 2021 functional communication/functional mutism **expert consensus**, whether and when it discusses electronic aids; ASHA alternative-communication guidance; Canadian counselling consent standards, application to a **17-year-old**, and the distinction between a therapy exercise and classroom access/accommodations. Claims about support “reinforcing” disability should not be upgraded from a plausible hypothesis to established evidence; remove no aid by editorial assumption.
4. **Functional seizure public safety:** Research on injuries and public-disability experiences, episode ambiguity/emergency response, and what empirical evidence (if any) exists about symptom-provoking reading. The clearly approved skip option helps choice but has **not been proven** to prevent seizures. Check for harm, preserving person dignity and avoiding bystander medical instructions.
5. **Adversity and treatment:** Ludwig 2018 group association research and the limitations of diagnosing individual causes, Mcloughlin 2025 iatrogenic-harm review and any cited AAN functional-seizure guidance. Audit the exact population and recommendation strength of each claim, not the age of a citation alone.
6. **Source integration:** The local Research and Sources tables are partly **legacy/generated**, with potentially unused Figure rows, newer inline research links and community quotes not necessarily mapped to page sources, plus Re-PROGRAM's incomplete citation record. Reconcile every retained inline claim, bibliography row, figure mapping, stable source ID and Reference Library link with the central index only after verifying originals.
