### Voice and editorial method refined during Module 11

**Across all audiences:** Start with an observable, relatable problem and reveal a genuine discovery through action and dialogue. Avoid retelling the same conclusion in narrative explanations immediately afterward. Keep connected paragraphs, purposeful dialogue and a crisp, earned ending. We learned that tightening some stories about 15–25% made them much clearer; treat that as a pacing principle, not a fixed word-count reduction. Briefly revive established terms in block quotes rather than reteaching whole mechanisms. Cases may end with uncertainty and continuing disability.

**For the Person With FND:** Accessible approximately age-14 reading level, warm but not childish, rich enough to recognize the lesson in a new situation. Develop a human story and decision, then explain the new concept without repetition or self-diagnosis. Distinguish observations, possibilities, evidence and individual causation. Recognize practical improvements and participation even when FND symptoms remain. Preserve optional short reflections (“Something to think about,” “How might that feel?” and “One small step”) when they add something.

**For Supporters:** Primarily **the supporter's own perspective**, as with Callum's father, Nadia's friend and Graham's friend Lena. Use the approachable **“advice over coffee”** voice, humane detail and occasional humour. The supporter's insight evolves, their limits and worries matter, and the person with FND retains privacy, voice and decisions. Don't make a good-intentioned supporter a villain or a substitute therapist. Dialogue should change the story, not explain neuroscience in a circle. If a communication pause occurs, it must serve the primary care decision rather than becoming a separate lesson. Include a genuinely relevant authentic supporter quote only when verified; don't force one.

**For Clinicians:** Address knowledgeable, thoughtful colleagues through **case-specific clinical reasoning**, not an introductory FND lecture. Show what the clinician knows, why options and competing interpretations matter, and how they choose a proportionate, individualized next step. Avoid sample notes/referral records masquerading as reasoning, rigid “What/Why/How” printed headings, overlong caution lists and stale mechanistic explanations. Distinguish clinical formulation, evidential findings, hypothesis about causation, comorbid mental-health care, patient's goals, referral purpose, outcome measures and real follow-up responsibility. A clinical case should be independently intelligible without retelling the Person section.

### Module 12's specific scientific and ethical boundaries

Psychological care may help with a **specific FND presentation**, the **experience of living with disability**, or a **coexisting mental-health condition**. Those are not interchangeable indications. An assessment, a course of therapy and a conclusion about the cause of FND are separate things. The effectiveness of therapy does not prove a causal theory, and lack of improvement doesn't imply patient resistance or dishonesty.

Trauma may matter for some people, but is not required to develop FND, to have FND diagnosed or to receive treatment. No compulsory trauma search, coerced disclosure or memories-as-proof narrative. Preserve consent, appropriate treatment fit, alternative care and the patient's right to pause or decline nonurgent intervention, while taking safety concerns seriously.

The psychological referral in Nadia's story has not addressed her worsening migraine care by itself. Keep migraine and other medical needs independently visible without condemning psychology. Clinicians, especially psychologists, must be credible rather than a stereotype of harm or magical efficacy.

Use original research and authoritative guidance, with accurate population, intervention, primary versus secondary outcomes and limits. In particular, the CODES functional-seizure CBT trial reported **no significant difference on its primary seizure-frequency endpoint** while some secondary measures favoured the intervention. Never summarize this as an unqualified cure or no effect. Existing generated lesson quote options require original-source checks; never invent or launder community quotations.

**Never place private disclosure documents, personal records, named assessors, or identifying history into this public repository.** Treat prior personal experiences only as de-identified inspiration when permission permits.
