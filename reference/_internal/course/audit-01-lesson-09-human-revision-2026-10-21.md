## Assessment

The approved rewrite is coherent and broadly evidence-aligned. It distinguishes a positive diagnosis from a biopsychosocial formulation, coexisting conditions from causal hypotheses, and participation from symptom reduction. Migraine receives separate care; the school and family examples preserve choice and individual variation. The four-question exercise separates observations, proposed explanations, alternatives and useful decisions. Retain the approved voice, softened-lighting classroom example and diverse symptom scope.

The principal corrections concern quotation accuracy/provenance and the site's citation rules. No major unsupported mechanism, universal treatment promise or requirement for psychological stress was identified. The historical October 17 reports were read as background, not as certification of this rewrite.
