## Scope and acceptance test

This coordinated change replaces the end-of-Person-section **Thought**, **Heart** and **Activity** prompts in **Lessons 1–29 and 33–35** (32 lessons). The three approved headings stay identical in every revised lesson:

1. `Something to think about` — recognize and apply the lesson's central idea.
2. `How might that feel?` — relate the idea to a value, need or feeling without prescribing an emotion.
3. `One small step` — one optional, accessible, low-demand observation, conversation, adaptation or action.

**Lessons 30–32, Module 10:** deliberately untouched.  
**Lessons 36–38, Module 12:** the nine project-lead-approved prompts from PR #210 are retained **verbatim**.  
**Lessons 39–59:** outside this review; their draft inventory entries remain reference material and are not represented as human-approved.

For each of the **35 applicable lessons**, the reviewer checked:

- The Person-section lesson story supplies any unfamiliar idea or term used in the prompts, or an earlier lesson has already taught it.
- The Thought/Heart/Activity trio teaches the central message of that lesson rather than testing unrelated knowledge, and adds to the course progression.
- Readers without the story character's specific symptoms can reflect using another situation, the lesson example, or an imagined alternative.
- No required diary, new therapy, particular clinician, mobility ability, supporter, seizure warning, improvement or unsafe self-experiment is presumed.
- The Activity is optional and does not present CBT reflection as an FND treatment protocol or imply thoughts cause involuntary symptoms.
- The complete content outside the end-question region remains identical to current `main` in each edited lesson; illustrations and other audience sections are preserved.

## Small changes required by the inclusion review

These are the only substantive refinements **beyond** the coordinated question proposal; all other proposed wording was implemented as approved.

| Lesson | Adjustment | Reason |
|---|---|---|

| 22 | Questions address activities as well as conversations | Includes non-speech and non-cognitive presentations |
| 23 | Thought concerns symptoms generally, not only non-cooperating movement | Includes non-movement FND |
