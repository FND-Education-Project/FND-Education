## Evidence verification and clinical interpretation

These are source-specific checks, not independent clinical endorsement of the course. No retained direct scientific quotation was found in these three lessons; research is paraphrased. Fictional scenes, cases and interpretive answer keys are educational illustrations, not study participants or proof of efficacy.

| Source | Independent check and evidence type | Relevant claim and limits |
|---|---|---|
| [Nicholson et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1136/jnnp-2019-322281), FND-CIT-0011 | Original publisher indexed text and original supplementary material; professional consensus. Full publisher page access was restricted. | Environment, routines, task demands, accommodations and meaningful participation. Does not prove isolated pacing strategies or the fictional cases’ outcomes. |
| [Australian National Mental Health Commission 2019 summary](https://www.mentalhealthcommission.gov.au/sites/default/files/2024-04/experiences-of-functional-neurological-disorder---summary-report.pdf), FND-CIT-0332 | Original 13-page report, especially pp. 9–10; self-selected consumer/carer survey. | Supports general carer health and practical burdens. 113 consumer and 66 carer respondents; not representative prevalence or proof every household is affected alike. |
| [Sandri et al.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00702-024-02829-4), FND-CIT-0347 | Original publisher full text, methods/results/limitations; cross-sectional study, 87 FGD and 48 healthy controls. | Selected gait differences across concurrent-task conditions. Not an instruction-recall experiment or treatment trial; selected ambulatory sample and exclusions limit generalization. Morgan’s case remains an individual fictional reasoning exercise. |
| [Wieling et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(22)00169-7), FND-CIT-0333 | Original abstract and indexed original review PDF sections. | Upright visual dimming can warrant history and blood-pressure assessment. Does not diagnose Morgan or attribute every standing symptom to orthostatic hypotension. |
| [AAN functional-seizure guideline](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41370742/), FND-CIT-0010 | Original abstract and indexed original recommendation sections; full guideline not independently appraised. A known FND diagnosis does not remove differential assessment. |
| [Cock & Edwards 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6334101/) | Original full text. Clinical review, Clin Med 18(5):414–417; DOI 10.7861/clinmedicine.18-5-414. | Awareness may persist without an observable response. Specific recommendation against sternal rub/nail-bed pressure/other noxious stimuli supports R1. Individual signs are not infallible diagnostic tests. Source is audit-only until R1 is approved. |
| [Official Glasgow Coma Scale guidance](https://www.glasgowcomascale.org/faq/) | Official structured assessment and FAQ. | GCS reports observed responses with important interfering factors; it does not establish the cause of unresponsiveness. No retrospective rescoring of David. Existing awareness discussion is retained. |
| [Bacchi, Verghese & Slee 2024](https://doi.org/10.5694/mja2.52249) | Original two-page MJA clinical education article, 220:364–365. | Acute safety and avoiding non-indicated treatment for functional seizures; other medical indications still matter. Not a rule that every unresponsive person with prior FND needs no urgent assessment. Audit-only corroboration. |
| [NICE NG206](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206/chapter/Recommendations), FND-CIT-0206 | Official indexed recommendation sections, including suspected ME/CFS and energy/activity management; direct page access restricted. | Delayed disproportionate worsening, assessment for other causes, avoiding fixed incremental exercise in ME/CFS.
