# Module 12: Autonomic and Whole-Body Symptoms

> **Working draft:** This page was automatically generated and is looking for [contributors and reviewers](https://fnd-education-project.github.io/FND-Education/).

Heart rate, blood pressure, sweating, temperature, digestion and bladder function are not controlled by willpower. Symptoms in these areas are real, but “autonomic dysregulation” is not one diagnosis or a complete explanation.

## What this module teaches

- The autonomic nervous system helps regulate automatic body functions.
- Dizziness, palpitations, nausea, bowel change, sweating and temperature difficulty can have many causes.
- Group-level autonomic findings in FND are mixed and are not an individual diagnostic test.
- Conditions such as POTS or orthostatic hypotension can coexist with FND and require their own criteria.
- A short symptom-and-context record can help; constant monitoring can also increase burden.
- A personal plan should separate daily access, agreed treatment and signs that need reassessment. (*citations* [1](#citation-1), [2](#citation-2), [3](#citation-3))
