This module separates noticing an activity pattern, making a flexible plan, and learning from delayed worsening.

## In this module

- Activity load can include physical, cognitive, sensory, emotional and practical demands.
- Pacing can protect essentials and valued life without becoming a rigid avoidance rule.
- A flare is information for care and review, not evidence of personal failure.

FND occupational-therapy guidance supports individualized planning. Pacing research borrowed from ME/CFS is mixed and must remain clearly labelled as non-FND-specific. (*citations* [1](#citation-1), [2](#citation-2))
