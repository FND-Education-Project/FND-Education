FND can change roles, plans, relationships and how a person sees themselves. These losses are real. There is no required way to grieve or rebuild.

## In this module

- Make room for grief without treating it as the cause of FND.
- Talk about intimacy, help and boundaries with consent and without blame.
- Include supporter wellbeing without making the ill person responsible for everyone.

Qualitative research reports loss, stigma, relationship strain, isolation, hope and purpose, but small samples cannot define what every person should feel. (*citations* [1](#citation-1), [2](#citation-2))
