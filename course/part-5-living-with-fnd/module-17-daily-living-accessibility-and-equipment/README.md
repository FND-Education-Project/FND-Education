This module is about making daily life safer and more possible **now**. Using an adaptation does not mean giving up on rehabilitation.

## In this module

- Break a difficult activity into smaller parts before deciding what may help.
- Judge an aid by the person's safety, access, independence, effort and goals—not by a blanket rule.
- Try and review equipment because bodies, homes and needs can change.

Clinical guidance supports individual occupational assessment. Falls research also shows why risk needs a broad assessment rather than one simple answer. The long-term effects of particular aids in FND have not been tested well enough for universal rules. (*citations* [1](#citation-1), [2](#citation-2))
