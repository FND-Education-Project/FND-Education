Paid work can matter deeply, but it is not a test of recovery or worth. Some people can continue with changes; some cannot work or study; some contribute in other ways.

## In this module

- Match accommodations to actual task barriers and variable capacity.
- Describe function in concrete terms without exaggerating or minimizing.
- Keep belonging, purpose and care available when paid work is not.

Work research in FND is limited and often concerns functional seizures or specialist samples. It nevertheless documents substantial work difficulty and disability; inability to work must not be treated as lack of effort. (*citations* [1](#citation-1), [2](#citation-2))
