# Module 1 — What Is Functional Neurological Disorder?

> **Working draft:** This page was automatically generated and is [looking for contributors and reviewers](https://fnd-education-project.github.io/FND-Education/).

A short starting point for understanding what FND is, what **functional** means, how symptoms can vary, and what improvement may mean.

## In this module

- FND symptoms are **real and involuntary**.
- FND should be diagnosed from **positive clinical features**, not simply because tests are normal.
- Psychological stress or a psychiatric condition is **not required** for an FND diagnosis.
- Biological, psychological, and social factors may affect symptoms differently for different people.
- FND can appear in many ways, and another medical or neurological condition can also be present.
- Improvement may mean remission, fewer symptoms, better function, greater independence, or better quality of life.
