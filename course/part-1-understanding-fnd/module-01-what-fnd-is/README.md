# Module 1 — What Is Functional Neurological Disorder?

> **Working draft:** This page was automatically generated and is [looking for contributors and reviewers](https://fnd-education-project.github.io/FND-Education/).

A short starting point for understanding what FND is, what **functional** means, and what remission, recovery, and other forms of improvement may mean.

## What this module teaches

- FND symptoms are **real and involuntary**.
- FND should be diagnosed from **positive clinical features**, not simply because tests are normal.
- Psychological stress or a psychiatric condition is **not required** for an FND diagnosis.
- Another medical or neurological condition can be present alongside FND.
- There is no cure for FND. Remission and recovery are explained as separate ideas.
- Symptoms may recur or worsen regardless of what a person knows, tries, or has achieved. Symptom severity is not a measure of effort, acceptance, treatment participation, or personal progress.
