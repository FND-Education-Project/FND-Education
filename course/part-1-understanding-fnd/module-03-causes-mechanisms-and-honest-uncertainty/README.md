There is not one complete explanation for why FND begins or continues in every person. This module separates what researchers have observed from what they have proposed, and it shows how to consider possible influences without turning them into blame or unsupported certainty.

## What this module teaches

- **Cause, risk factor, trigger, mechanism and maintaining factor do not mean the same thing.** (*citations* [1](#citation-1), [3](#citation-3), [4](#citation-4))
- The event near the beginning of symptoms may differ from what affects symptoms now. (*citations* [1](#citation-1), [3](#citation-3))
- Physical, psychological and social factors may have different roles in different people. (*citations* [3](#citation-3), [4](#citation-4))
- Trauma, stress and mental illness are not required for an FND diagnosis. (*citations* [1](#citation-1))
- Brain-network and predictive-processing research offers possible explanations, not one settled cause or individual biomarker. (*citations* [1](#citation-1), [2](#citation-2), [5](#citation-5))
- A useful treatment or explanation does not automatically prove why FND began. (*citations* [1](#citation-1), [3](#citation-3))
- Research claims can be judged by what was measured, who was studied, what remains uncertain and whether the result applies to an individual. (*citations* [3](#citation-3), [4](#citation-4), [5](#citation-5))
