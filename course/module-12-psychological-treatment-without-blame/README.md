This module separates why psychological care may be offered, how to choose a therapy, and how consent and trauma should be handled.

## In this module

- Psychological treatment does not mean FND is imagined or caused by stress.
- A therapy should have an agreed target, reasonable fit and a way to review benefit or harm.
- Trauma is relevant for some people and not required for FND diagnosis or treatment.

Research differs by therapy, FND presentation and outcome. Group associations with adversity do not establish an individual's cause or make trauma treatment compulsory. (*citations* [1](#citation-1), [2](#citation-2), [3](#citation-3))
