This final module asks what changed, what still needs attention and what you want next. It is not a pass-or-fail test.

## In this module

- notice progress across understanding, safety, access, daily life and symptoms; and
- choose a small next step while recording non-response, harm and unmet needs honestly.

FND outcome-measure research supports looking across several domains and keeping the person's experience central. No single measure defines recovery, effort or worth. (*citations* [1](#citation-1), [2](#citation-2))
