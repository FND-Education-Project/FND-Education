FND can explain familiar symptoms without explaining every future symptom. This module helps you compare what is happening now with your usual pattern, prepare brief emergency information and use an individual safety plan.

## What this module teaches

- A known FND diagnosis does not prevent another illness or injury. (*citations* [1](#citation-1), [2](#citation-2))
- A familiar event and a new or meaningfully changed event need different judgment.
- A short, clinician-reviewed plan can guide other people during familiar events. (*citation* [3](#citation-3))
- Good care avoids both automatic dismissal and unnecessary harmful treatment. (*citations* [1](#citation-1), [2](#citation-2))
