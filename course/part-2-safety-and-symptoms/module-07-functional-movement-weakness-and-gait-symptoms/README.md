Movement symptoms can look and feel very different from one another. This module gives each main pattern its own short page, then explains movement retraining separately.

## What this module teaches

- Positive signs can show that movement works differently across tasks; they do not show that symptoms are voluntary. (*citation* [1](#citation-1))
- Weakness, tremor, jerks, dystonia, gait problems and falls need pattern-specific assessment.
- Aids and accessibility can support life while rehabilitation is considered.
- Movement retraining may use meaningful automatic movement and redirected attention, with a dose suited to the person. (*citations* [2](#citation-2), [3](#citation-3))
