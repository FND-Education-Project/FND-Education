We're building free, evidence-informed FND education, and we'd love help making it clearer, more useful and easier to use. **People living with FND are especially welcome**, along with family members, other supporters, clinicians and anyone who has a skill they'd like to share.

## Skills that can help

You don't need to do everything or commit to a big project. Even one thoughtful suggestion or a small task can help.

- **Lived experience and user feedback:** Read a page or try an activity and tell us what is clear, confusing or difficult to use.
- **Research and source checking:** Find reliable information, check references and help us explain what is known or uncertain.
- **Writing and editing:** Make explanations easier to follow, or help with spelling, structure and plain language.
- **Clinical review:** Clinicians and researchers can check accuracy, safety and practical guidance within their areas of expertise.
- **Accessibility and website testing:** Look for difficulties with navigation, readability, screen readers, mobile devices or sensory demands.
- **Creative and technical work:** Help with illustrations, diagrams, short videos, design, coding or testing.

## People with FND are welcome

Living with FND can give you a valuable perspective on what readers need. You might notice a wording problem, suggest a gentler way to present an activity, or bring skills from your work or interests. You don't have to share your personal health story to take part.

We understand that energy, symptoms and available time can change. You can suggest one small thing without taking on an ongoing commitment.

## What matters to us

We value kindness, clear communication and respect for different experiences. Health information needs to follow reliable evidence, and we should be open about what research has not yet established. Clinical guidance is checked by people with relevant expertise.

**We're asking for time, skills or feedback—not financial donations.** The educational resources will remain free for readers.
