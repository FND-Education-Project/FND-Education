## We’re looking for contributors and reviewers

Help may include research, writing, reviewing, videos, accessibility work, or artwork.

### What we ask

- Communicate kindly, clearly and conversationally.
- Enjoy helping people learn.
- Follow the research and say honestly when something is uncertain.
- Offer only the time you can comfortably give.

### About the project

The finished resource is being built to be written and reviewed by people with FND, their caregivers and supporters, and the clinicians who support them.

It will remain free for readers. Contributors volunteer their time.

### Interested?

Email us and briefly tell us what you would enjoy helping with. A short note is enough.
