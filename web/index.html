FND EDUCATION PROJECT

Understand FND. Find practical help. Follow the evidence.

Free, evidence-informed education about Functional Neurological Disorder for people living with FND, family and other supporters, and the clinicians who work with them.

Working educational resource: This project is still being reviewed and expanded. It provides general education, not individual medical advice, diagnosis, emergency assessment, or treatment.

Look up a symptom Use the Reference Library to find symptom descriptions, diagnostic information, recovery approaches, and common co-occurring conditions. Check a term Use the glossary for plain-language explanations of clinical, research, rehabilitation, and FND terminology.

What this project is trying to do

FND information is spread across research papers, clinical guidance, specialist services, patient organizations, and lived experience. This project brings those strands together while keeping the source and strength of a claim visible.

For people with FND, the goal is to make the condition easier to understand, discuss, and manage. For supporters, it is to make practical help easier to give without blame, pressure, or loss of autonomy. For clinicians, the project aims to connect research and examination findings with the person's actual symptoms, function, participation, and everyday life.

How pages are written

Educational pages distinguish research findings, clinical guidance, practical rehabilitation ideas, and lived experience. Where evidence is limited or uncertain, the page should say so. FND can also coexist with other neurological or medical conditions, so the project does not assume that every new or changing symptom is caused by FND.
