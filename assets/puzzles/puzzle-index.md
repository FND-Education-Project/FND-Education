# Puzzle Index

This index connects each printable puzzle with the course material it supports. Difficulty labels are general guides; a person may choose whichever version feels most manageable.

## Module 1: What FND Is

### What is FND?

Reviews words used to describe FND, the nervous system, symptoms and improvement. It supports the [Module 1 overview](../../course/part-1-understanding-fnd/module-01-what-fnd-is/README.md) and its first lesson, [What “Functional” Means and How FND Can Appear](../../course/part-1-understanding-fnd/module-01-what-fnd-is/01-what-functional-means-and-how-fnd-can-appear.md).

- [Easy crossword with word bank](crosswords/easy/what-is-fnd.pdf)
- [Normal crossword](crosswords/normal/what-is-fnd.pdf)
- [Answer key](crosswords/answer-keys/what-is-fnd-answer-key.pdf)
- [Expanded normal crossword](crosswords/normal/what-is-fnd-expanded.pdf) — an earlier version with additional clues.
- [Expanded answer key](crosswords/answer-keys/what-is-fnd-expanded-answer-key.pdf)

### Biopsychosocial What?

Reviews the body-and-brain, thoughts-and-feelings, and social parts of a person's circumstances without treating them as three required causes. It supports [The Biopsychosocial Model, Misconceptions, and Recovery](../../course/part-1-understanding-fnd/module-01-what-fnd-is/02-the-biopsychosocial-model-misconceptions-and-recovery.md).

- [Easy crossword with word bank](crosswords/easy/biopsychosocial-what.pdf)
- **Normal crossword:** not yet available.
- [Answer key](crosswords/answer-keys/biopsychosocial-what-answer-key.pdf)

### Recovery

Reviews remission, setbacks, function, safety, hope and different meanings of improvement. It supports [Remission, Recovery, and What Improvement Can Mean](../../course/part-1-understanding-fnd/module-01-what-fnd-is/03-remission-recovery-and-what-improvement-can-mean.md).

- [Easy crossword with word bank](crosswords/easy/recovery.pdf)
- [Normal crossword](crosswords/normal/recovery.pdf)
- [Answer key](crosswords/answer-keys/recovery-answer-key.pdf)
