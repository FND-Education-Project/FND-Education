## Evidence and limits

Targeted review, not a systematic review: IFCN 2024 (0022), the general myoclonus review (0151), Beudel 2018 (0122), Zutt 2017 mood comparison (new 0225) and MDS study-group 2025 review (new 0226) were accessible in full. Van der Salm 2012 (0125) was checked at primary abstract/institutional-record level; full text remains outstanding. Other inherited inventory sources have not all received a fresh full-text review.

The draft distinguishes a slow BP from a brief cortical spike, and absent BP from an inadequate recording. BP does not establish conscious intention and overlaps with some tics. The 2012 abstract's accuracy figures for intentional wrist extension are not reused as spontaneous-jerk accuracy. Beudel's 14/29 versus 0/16 finding is reported with retrospective selection, incorporation bias and limited comparator cautions. Mood does not identify cause; diagnostic evidence does not measure disability.
